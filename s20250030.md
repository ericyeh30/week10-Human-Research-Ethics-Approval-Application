2. Project Overview
Research Objectives

The overall aim of this research is to investigate people's awareness, experience, perceptions, and concerns regarding AI-generated video and audio.

The specific objectives are:

To examine participants' awareness and familiarity with AI-generated video and audio.
To investigate participants' experience with AI-generated video and audio tools.
To understand participants' perceptions of the quality and usefulness of AI-generated video and audio.
To examine participants' perceptions of the efficiency of AI-generated video and audio in digital content creation.
To identify ethical concerns associated with AI-generated video and audio, including misinformation, deepfakes, copyright issues, and misuse.
To understand participants' views regarding the future impact of AI-generated video and audio on digital content creation.
Research Questions/Hypotheses

The research will address the following questions:

What is the level of awareness and familiarity with AI-generated video and audio among participants?
What experience do participants have with AI-generated video and audio tools?
How do participants perceive the quality and usefulness of AI-generated video and audio?
How do participants perceive the efficiency of AI-generated video and audio in digital content creation?
What ethical concerns do participants have regarding AI-generated video and audio?
How do participants perceive the future impact of AI-generated video and audio on digital content creation?

No formal hypothesis will be tested because the study is primarily exploratory and descriptive in nature.

3. Methodology
Research Design

This study will use a primarily quantitative, cross-sectional survey design, with one open-ended question providing limited qualitative feedback.

An online questionnaire will be used to collect participants' responses regarding their awareness, experience, perceptions, ethical concerns, and views about the future impact of AI-generated video and audio.

The survey will contain closed-ended questions using categorical and Likert-scale response options, together with one open-ended question.

Participant Recruitment

Participants will be recruited through appropriate online communication channels, such as personal networks, university-related networks, and social media or online communication platforms where appropriate.

Participation will be voluntary. Potential participants will receive information about the purpose of the study and what participation involves before completing the survey.

The study will not involve coercion or pressure to participate. Participants will not receive financial or other significant incentives for participation.

The study will recruit participants who are 18 years of age or older.

Sample Size

The study plans to recruit approximately 50–100 participants, depending on the availability and willingness of eligible participants.

The sample is intended for an academic student research project and is not intended to represent the entire population.

Data Collection Methods

Data will be collected through an online questionnaire consisting of 11 questions divided into five sections:

Section A – Participant Background: Questions about age group and frequency of digital media use.
Section B – Awareness and Experience: Questions about familiarity with and experience using AI-generated video and audio.
Section C – Perceptions of AI-Generated Content: Questions about the quality, usefulness, and efficiency of AI-generated content.
Section D – Ethical Issues and Future Impact: Questions about ethical concerns and the future significance of AI-generated video and audio.
Section E – Open-Ended Feedback: One open-ended question asking participants about the biggest benefit or concern regarding AI-generated video and audio.

The survey is expected to take approximately 5–10 minutes to complete.

The questionnaire will avoid requesting unnecessary personally identifiable information.

Data Analysis Plan

The closed-ended survey responses will be analysed using descriptive statistical methods, including frequencies, percentages, and where appropriate, means or other summary statistics for Likert-scale responses.

The results will be presented using appropriate tables and charts to identify patterns in participants' awareness, experience, perceptions, ethical concerns, and views regarding future impact.

Responses to the open-ended question will be reviewed and grouped into common themes, such as benefits, efficiency, misinformation, deepfakes, copyright, and other ethical concerns.

The analysis will focus on overall patterns in the responses rather than identifying individual participants.

4. Ethical Considerations
4.1 Voluntary and Informed Participation

Participants will be provided with clear and accessible information about the research before beginning the survey.

The participant information will explain:

the purpose of the research;
what participation involves;
the approximate time required;
how the collected information will be used;
that participation is voluntary; and
participants' rights regarding participation and withdrawal.

Informed consent will be obtained before participants begin the questionnaire. Participants will be asked to confirm that they understand the information provided and agree to participate.

Participants will not be required to provide a reason for choosing not to participate.

Participants will be informed that they can stop completing the survey at any time before submitting their responses. Where responses have already been submitted anonymously and cannot reasonably be linked back to an individual, withdrawal of an individual response may not be possible. This limitation will be explained to participants before participation.

4.2 Minimising Harm and Discomfort

The research is expected to involve minimal risk because participation consists of completing an online survey about AI-generated video and audio.

There are no expected physical risks associated with participation.

Some participants may experience minor discomfort when considering ethical issues such as misinformation, deepfakes, copyright infringement, or misuse of AI-generated content. To minimise potential discomfort, participants will be informed about the general nature of the questions before completing the survey.

Participants will not be required to disclose sensitive personal information or provide detailed information about private experiences.

Participants may skip questions where the survey design allows this or stop participating if they feel uncomfortable.

4.3 Protecting Confidentiality and Privacy

The study will minimise the collection of personally identifiable information.

Participants will not be asked to provide unnecessary identifying information such as their full name, home address, or other sensitive personal details.

Survey responses will be treated confidentially and will be reported in aggregated form wherever possible.

If demographic information is reported, it will only be presented in a way that does not reasonably identify individual participants.

Access to research data will be limited to authorised members of the student research group and the project supervisor where necessary.

The researchers will not attempt to identify individual participants from their responses.

4.4 Ensuring Fair and Respectful Treatment

All eligible participants will be treated fairly and respectfully.

Participation will not be restricted based on gender, nationality, cultural background, or personal opinions, provided that participants meet the study's eligibility requirements.

The survey questions will use neutral and respectful language and will avoid deliberately leading participants toward a particular opinion about AI-generated video and audio.

The research team will avoid making assumptions about participants' knowledge, experience, or attitudes toward artificial intelligence.

The researchers will also acknowledge that participants may have different levels of familiarity with AI technology and will ensure that the survey provides appropriate response options for participants with different levels of experience.

4.5 Honesty with Professional Colleagues

The research findings will be reported honestly and transparently.

The researchers will not fabricate, alter, or intentionally misrepresent survey responses.

All relevant findings, including findings that do not support the researchers' expectations, will be reported appropriately.

The research team will clearly distinguish between collected data, interpretation of the findings, and conclusions.

Contributions made by group members will be appropriately acknowledged. The guidance and supervision provided by Dr. Sudath Heiyanthuduwage will also be acknowledged where appropriate.

Participants will not be individually identified in the final research report.

5. Data Management
Data Storage

The collected survey data will be stored electronically in secure, password-protected storage.

Where the survey platform provides secure data storage, the collected responses will be stored within the platform and subsequently transferred to an authorised research storage location if required.

Research files used for analysis will only be shared among authorised members of the research group and the project supervisor where necessary.

The data will be retained for the period required by the relevant Sydney Institute of Higher Education research and data-management requirements.

Data Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures will be used to protect the research data.

These measures will include:

password-protected accounts and devices;
restricting access to authorised members of the research group;
avoiding unnecessary collection of identifying information;
not publicly sharing individual participant responses; and
reporting results in aggregated or de-identified form.

Any research files containing participant information will not be shared publicly on social media or other public platforms.

Data Disposal

After the required retention period, electronic research data and working files containing participant information will be securely deleted from the relevant storage systems.

Any physical documents containing personal information, if produced, will be securely destroyed.

Data disposal will be conducted in a manner intended to prevent unauthorised access or recovery of participant information.
